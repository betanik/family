### Photo Organization Tips:
1. The compilation photo uses labeled boxes with family member names
2. You can cross-reference names with the **Interactive Family Tree**
3. Look for generational markers (C1, C7, etc.) to understand relationships
4. Family group photos show spouse relationships and multiple generations together

### Contributing More Photos:
If you have additional photos to add:
- Individual portraits (label with generation marker like "C1 Name")
- Family groups (label with all members shown)
- Ancestral photos (label as "P0", "P1", "P2" etc.)
- Upload to the `/photos` folder with clear naming
